- New for administrators: switch Live TV off for one account (Admin, Users, the account's page). That account no longer sees Live TV and can't stream it. Needs Aurora Guard 1.61.0.
- Fixed: on the TV, an administrator's warnings and outage notices now stay on screen, and information notices stay 45 seconds and return the next time Aurora opens. Administrators also see every announcement, including ones aimed at one plan or reseller. Needs Aurora Guard 1.60.2 for the second part.
- New: if your reseller gave you a code, choose "Have a code from your reseller?" on the sign-in screen and type it. The sign-in screen then shows your reseller's name, welcome and how to reach them. Needs Aurora Guard 1.60.0.
- Fixed: a movie that played for some people but showed "Playback failed: The browser could not play this file" for others now plays. When a browser or the Windows app can't play a file as it is, Aurora asks the server to convert it automatically.
- New for resellers: your own customer page. In Sales tools, copy a link that shows your business name, welcome and contact details to your customers on the sign-in and setup pages. Needs Aurora Guard 1.60.0.
- New for customers: Home now starts with a Tonight section: your reseller's name and welcome, a game that is on or about to start (a team you follow first), how many days your plan has left with a Renew button, and your reseller's offers.
- New for resellers: Pulse at the top of Overview (watching now, income, customers, credits) and one-tap WhatsApp reminders for customers who are ending. Needs Aurora Guard 1.59.0 for the announcement.
- Fixed: the Sports tab now finds sports channels (ESPN, FOX, NBC, TNT and more) even when your provider keeps them in a group that is not named for sports, instead of saying a game is not on your channels.
- Resellers: choose Live TV groups (like pay-per-view) to keep off your free trials, in Settings. Needs Aurora Guard 1.58.0.
- Resellers: the Reseller panel sections are now in a menu button, and a "What's new" pop-up tells you once what changed after each update. Needs Aurora Guard 1.57.0.
- New for resellers: a customer health list (who is buffering, quiet, owing or just ended), helpers with their own limited logins, pausing a customer until a date, scheduled renewals and messages, bulk actions, and their own business name and welcome text for their customers. Administrators choose what is on in Admin, Resellers. Needs Aurora Guard 1.56.0.
- Resellers: the reseller rules were updated and every reseller has to agree again. The Reseller panel shows what is new in short, so there is no need to read the whole page. Needs Aurora Guard 1.55.0.
- New: Admin, Users now has tabs that separate your own customers from your resellers' customers (with a picker for one reseller), and a Staff and resellers tab.
- New for resellers: choose their own renewal reminder days, show customers how to pay (and let them say they paid), offer longer plans like 12 months at a better price, and reward customers who bring friends with free days or a coupon. Administrators set what is on for everyone in Admin, Resellers. Needs Aurora Guard 1.54.0.
- New: administrators can lift the daily free trial limit for chosen resellers (Admin, Resellers, Edit). Needs Aurora Guard 1.53.0.
- New: provider Live TV channels now play in a web browser and in the Windows app (needs Aurora Guard 1.52.0). Some channels use sound or video a browser cannot play; Android plays those.
- New: Aurora for Windows laptops and PCs (download from the Aurora website), and Aurora can now be installed from a web browser too. Resellers have setup guides for both.
- The Admin PIN now also locks Admin → Users, next to Resellers and credit requests. Needs Aurora Guard 1.36.0.
- New: add an administrator or a moderator at any time from Admin → Users ("Admins and moderators"), and remove them again. Who is an administrator is now decided by Emby's administrator switch, not a fixed list of names. Needs Aurora Guard 1.35.0.
- New: Reseller PIN. Lock Admin → Resellers and credit requests behind a 4-digit PIN so another administrator can't open them. Set it in Admin → Resellers. Needs Aurora Guard 1.34.0.
- New for resellers: see a customer's playback quality (is it their internet, their settings or the provider?), see and remove a single device, send a notice to their own customers, and set contact details (WhatsApp, phone, email) that their customers see under Help & support. Needs Aurora Guard 1.33.0.
- New: Playback quality in Admin → Users → a customer. See how much they buffer, how fast channels start, their stream speed and device, next to everyone else's average, with what to try. Needs Aurora Guard 1.32.0; the numbers arrive after the customer updates the app.
- New: resellers can see what their customers are watching right now. A "Watching now" card and a line on each customer show the movie, show or live channel and the device, updated every 20 seconds (needs Aurora Guard 1.31.0). Resellers see only their own customers.
- New: backup streams. A channel can have up to 3 backups (another provider's copy of the same channel, or a pasted link). If the main one keeps failing, the Android, Fire TV and iPhone players switch to the next backup by themselves, and try the main one again next time the channel is opened. Add them in the channel editor on the website.
- New: a channel editor on the website for administrators and moderators (the same as Admin → Channels in the app), at /channels/ on the Aurora website.
- Live TV: channel names in the guide's left column are now shown in full (wider column, two lines, bigger text), and the details panel names the channel in large type. The category list on the Channels tab is wider too.
- New: Admin → Channels (admins and moderators). Add your own categories and channels, import an M3U playlist, and fix the provider's channels: rename, new picture, number, guide ID, another stream address, or hide. Changes reach every customer's Live TV within a few minutes.
- New: moderators. In Admin → Users → a customer, make them a moderator. A moderator gets an Admin menu with support messages, announcements, requests, channel reports, sports, and read-only problems, feedback, activity, usage and sharing. Users, Resellers, credits, Server health, updates, plans and the TV provider login stay admin-only.
- New: renewal reminders. Choose, per customer, how many days before the end they get the in-app reminder (with a default for everyone), and copy a ready-made message to send them. Resellers also pick how many days ahead their daily "ending soon" list covers.
- New on iPhone: Live TV channels play in a full-screen player, with swipe up and down to change channel and a channel list.
- Removed: the Review account switch in Admin → Users. Every account sees the same app.
