Questions people ask

Quick answers to the things people ask most.

What is Aurora?

Aurora is a streaming app for movies, TV shows and live TV. It looks and feels like the big streaming services and runs on your phone, your Android or Google TV and a web browser.

Which devices does it work on?

Android phones and tablets, Android TV and Google TV, and any modern web browser. Offline downloads are on the Android app.

How do I get an account?

Use the Sign up form on this page. Pick a username and password and your account is ready straight away. Then sign in on any of your devices.

How do I install Aurora on my TV?

Install the Downloader app from the Play Store, open it, type the code 2873179 and select Go. Choose Install, then Open, and sign in.

Where do I put a referral or discount code?

Enter it in the optional boxes on the sign-up form. A referral code from a friend gives you both 7 free days.

What is a connection?

One device playing at the same time. With 2 connections, two devices can play together. If a third tries, Aurora tells you to stop playback on another one first.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new account starts with 2 free days and no card is needed.

Can I watch live sports?

Yes. Live TV has a Sports tab with scores, your teams and the best channel for each game. Scores come from a public sports feed and can be a minute behind.

Can I watch without internet?

On the Android app you can download movies and episodes over Wi-Fi and watch them offline. Downloads stop working when an account expires.

A channel will not load. What now?

Open Settings, then Status, to check your connection and the service. Use Report on the channel. If many people report it, we see it right away.

Is there a kids mode?

Yes. Turn it on for a device in Settings, choose the age group, and set a PIN. Only titles with an allowed rating show, and Live TV and Settings are closed.

How do updates work?

The Android app checks for a new version and offers to install it, so you always have the latest features.

How do I renew or cancel?

Renew any time before your plan ends. Nothing renews by itself, so there is nothing to cancel.

Questions before you start?

Send us a message and we will help.
