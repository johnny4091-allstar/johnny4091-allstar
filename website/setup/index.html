Setup guide

Getting started

Set up Aurora on your device

Pick your device. You will need the username and password you were given.

Fire TV / Android TVAndroid phoneiPhone / iPadOlder devicesWeb

Fire TV Stick or Android TV

  1. On the TV, open the app store and install the free Downloader app.
  2. Open Downloader and type the code 3643447, then press Go.
  3. Choose Install, then Open.
  4. Sign in with your username and password.

If the TV asks, allow Downloader to install apps in Settings.

Android phone or tablet

  1. Download the Aurora app.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Allow "install from this source" if asked.
  3. Open Aurora and sign in with your username and password.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Install the free TestFlight app from the App Store.
  2. Open the Aurora TestFlight link on the iPhone.
  3. Tap Accept, then Install, then Open.
  4. Sign in with your username and password.

Aurora Lite (older devices)

For Android 7 devices and the Fire TV Stick 4K (1st generation).

  1. In the Downloader app, type aurora-streaming.org/lite and press Go.
  2. Choose Install, then Open.
  3. Sign in with your username and password.

Watch in a web browser

  1. Open aurora-streaming.org and choose the web app.
  2. Sign in with your username and password.
