Reseller help center

Quick answers for running your customers on Aurora. Back to your panel.

Customers

How do I add a customer?

Open Add customer, type a username and full name, pick a plan and press Create. The plan's credits are used. You get a ready-to-send welcome message with the login.

How do I add many customers at once?

In Add customer, open Import many customers. Paste rows from a spreadsheet (Full name, Email, Phone, Note) or choose a CSV file, preview it, then Import. Up to 50 at a time. Copy all logins when it finishes.

How do I renew someone?

Open the customer and press Renew, or use the filters (Ending soon, Due in 14 days) and renew everyone shown. Each renewal uses the plan's credits.

What are labels?

Private tags only you see, like Family or VIP. Open a customer, press Labels, and filter the list by label. You can also message everyone with a label.

How do I see who stopped watching?

Use the Inactive 14 days filter in Customers. It lists customers who have not signed in for two weeks. Send them a friendly message from Messages.

What is the customer timeline?

Press Timeline on a customer to see when they were created, renewed, paid, messaged and more.

Money

How do I track payments?

Open Money and record a payment: customer, amount, method, and whether it is paid or still owed. Mark owed amounts as paid later. This is your own record. Aurora does not take payments.

What is the credits forecast?

It adds up the credits you will need for customers who end in the next 30 days, and tells you if you are short, so you can ask for more credits in time.

Are the income numbers exact?

No. They are based on the prices you enter and the payments you record.

Selling

How do coupons work?

In Sales tools, make a code such as WELCOME10 (percent or money off, optional end date and use limit). Send it to a customer. They enter it on their account page when asking for more time, and you see the discounted price on their request.

How does the trial link work?

Turn it on in Sales tools and share the link. People can start one free trial each. Trials are limited per day and per person.

Can I set my own prices?

Yes. Enter what you charge for each plan in Sales tools. It is used for the money pages and coupons.

Messages and support

How do I message customers?

Open Messages, choose who (everyone, ending soon, a label...), write up to 300 characters and send. They see it in their Aurora inbox for 30 days. You can send up to 10 messages an hour.

Do Live TV channels play in a web browser or the Windows app?

Yes, if your provider's Aurora Guard is version 1.52.0 or newer. The Guard passes the channels on, so the video travels through the provider's PC and internet. A few channels use sound or video a browser cannot play (some send AC-3 sound or old MPEG-2 video). Those play in the Aurora app for Android. The TV guide with what is on now is only in the Android app.

How do renewal reminders work?

Your customers get a reminder in their Aurora inbox before their plan ends (7, 3 and 1 days by default). Each reminder has a Renew button that sends you a request. Change the days or switch reminders off in Settings.

How do customers know how to pay me?

Write your payment instructions and an optional pay link in Settings. Customers see them when they renew and can tick "I have already paid". You see that on their request, and approving it adds the payment to your payment record.

Can I sell 6 or 12 months at a discount?

Yes. In Sales tools, make an offer: pick a plan, how many times it repeats, what the customer pays and (optionally) how many credits it uses. Customers pick it when they renew, and you approve it like any request.

How do referral rewards work?

A customer gives a friend their code (on their account page, Refer a friend). When the friend uses it, you see it in Sales tools and choose to give the reward (free days or a discount coupon) with one tap, or dismiss it. Nothing is given until you do.

Can customers use Aurora on a Windows laptop?

Yes. Send them the Windows laptop or PC guide from the Guides tab. They download the installer, open it and sign in. They can also use Aurora in a web browser.

How do I send setup instructions?

Open Guides, pick the device, and copy the instructions into a message. Or copy the link to the public setup page.

A customer reported a problem. Where is it?

Open Support. You can reply and mark it solved. Channel problems from your customers are listed there too.

Rules and fixing problems

Where are the reseller rules?

On the rules page. You must accept them before you can make changes. Warnings and suspensions follow those rules.

It says the action is busy.

Wait a few seconds and try again. Only one credit action runs at a time on your account.

I see "Could not load. Your provider may need to update Aurora Guard".

A new feature needs the latest Aurora Guard. Ask the owner to update it.

I forgot my password.

Ask the owner to reset it for you.
