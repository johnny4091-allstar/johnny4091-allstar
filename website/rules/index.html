Reseller rules and regulations

These are the rules every Aurora reseller agrees to. They keep the service fair for customers, safe for resellers and sustainable for everyone. Aurora Guard enforces many of them automatically.

1Your agreement with Aurora

These rules apply to every Aurora reseller and sub-reseller. By signing in to the reseller panel and pressing I have read and agree, you accept them. Aurora records the date you agreed. Until you agree, the panel lets you look but not make changes.

When the rules change, the version date at the top of this page changes and you are asked to agree again. Keep using the panel only if you accept the new version.

2Credits and payments

  • Credits are bought in advance. Aurora does not work on credit: a reseller cannot add or renew a customer if the cost is more than the credits they hold, and the Guard refuses it.
  • Credits that have already been used to add or renew a customer are not refunded.
  • Ask for more credits from the panel. The owner reviews each credit request. Payment details are agreed directly with the owner and are never taken inside the app.
  • You are responsible for collecting payment from your own customers. Aurora does not collect it for you and cannot chase it for you.
  • You get an alert when your credits run low. Keeping enough credits for renewals is your job.

3Adding and looking after customers

  • Give every customer their own username and password. Never hand out your reseller login.
  • Free trials are limited to 5 a day per reseller. Trials are for people who are really thinking of subscribing, not for giving free access to friends again and again.
  • Choose the plan and Live TV categories that match what the customer paid for. Do not give a customer more than they paid for.
  • Aurora is not for children under 13. Do not add accounts for them, and do not collect or keep customers' personal details you do not need.
  • Answer your customers' messages and renewal requests promptly. Approve or deny each extension request in the panel instead of leaving it waiting.
  • Keep your customers' details private. Do not share them with anyone outside Aurora.

4Conduct and support

  • Be honest with customers about what Aurora is and what it costs. Do not promise things Aurora does not offer.
  • Be polite. Abusive, threatening or discriminating behaviour toward customers, other resellers or the Aurora team is not accepted.
  • You are the first line of support for your customers. Use the Help details in your panel so they can reach you, and ask the Aurora team only for what you cannot fix yourself.
  • Do not say you speak for Aurora, and do not use the Aurora name or logo in a way that suggests you own the service.
  • Do not copy, rebuild or resell Aurora's apps, website or Guard, and do not share the provider's login or stream addresses.

5Pricing and customer handling

  • You set the price you charge your own customers. You pay Aurora the credit cost of the plan you give them.
  • One account is for one household. Do not sell a single account to several unrelated people, and do not resell accounts you did not create in the panel.
  • A customer's complaint is yours to try to settle first. If you cannot, pass it on to the Aurora team with what you know.
  • If a customer is switched off or suspended by the Guard, tell them why in plain words. Do not give them a way around the rules.

6What Aurora Guard enforces automatically

The Guard is strict on purpose. These rules apply to every customer account, and you cannot switch them off for your customers.

RuleWhat the Guard does
Device limitEach account has a number of devices allowed at the same time (the plan's number). A device counts as in use if it was active in the last 5 minutes. Over the limit, the Guard signs out the newest devices and tells the customer why.
End dateWhen an account's end date passes, the Guard switches the account off. It switches it back on when you extend it.
Paused too longA movie or show paused for 2 hours is stopped and the device is signed out, which frees the connection. Live TV is not affected.
Idle devicesA device not used for 30 days is signed out. The customer simply signs in again.
Sign-in lockAfter 5 wrong passwords in a row, the website sign-in for that account is locked for 15 minutes.
Account sharingThe Guard watches for accounts used from many places. It flags them to the owner for review. Sharing an account across households can lead to the account being suspended.
CreditsAn add or renewal that costs more than your credits is refused. Nothing can take your credits below zero.
TrialsNo more than 5 free trials a day per reseller.
Adult channelsHidden unless the account has the adult switch on, and then behind a PIN. They never appear in the iPhone or Apple TV apps.
Your panelYour actions are written to an audit log with your name and the time. Changes to the reseller rules need your agreement before you can make any change.

7Keeping your reseller account safe

  • Use a long, private password and change it if you think someone else knows it.
  • Never share your reseller login or let someone else work in your panel under your name.
  • Tell the owner at once if you think your account was misused or a customer's details leaked.
  • You are responsible for everything done under your login.

8Warnings and suspension

If you break these rules, the owner can give you a written warning from the Admin panel. You see every warning, with the reason, at the top of your panel.

  • You can have 3 warnings at the same time. Each warning counts for 90 days, then it drops off.
  • A fourth warning while three are still active suspends your reseller account automatically. The owner is alerted.
  • While suspended, you cannot make changes. Your customers keep their access unless the owner decides otherwise.
  • Serious breaches (for example sharing the reseller login, selling a stolen or shared account, or abusing customers) can lead to immediate suspension or removal, with or without earlier warnings.
  • To appeal a warning or a suspension, contact the owner directly and explain what happened. The owner decides, and the owner can reinstate you or remove a warning.
